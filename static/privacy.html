Privacy Policy

Effective September 23, 2026

1. Scope

This Privacy Policy explains how Build-a-Wallet handles information when you use buildawallet.xyz, the HUMAN wallet-design experience, the NON-HUMAN agent infrastructure, API, documentation, MCP endpoint and related services.

2. Information we may process

Depending on how you use the service, we may process account or credential identifiers, API request metadata, wallet configuration data, public blockchain addresses, transaction request data, policy settings, device/browser information, logs, support communications and billing-related information if paid services are enabled.

Build-a-Wallet is designed so private keys are not returned through public HTTP APIs. Public blockchain addresses and on-chain transaction information are inherently public and may be visible to third parties.

3. HUMAN wallet builder data

The HUMAN builder may process the wallet preferences you submit, including selected assets, networks, custody preferences, security features, interface settings and optional contact information. Saved public blueprints may be displayed in gallery or sharing features when you choose to make them public.

4. NON-HUMAN / API data

Agent infrastructure may process scoped credential identifiers, wallet authority records, policy settings, audit events, transaction requests, execution results, chain RPC responses and pricing data needed to enforce user-defined policies.

5. How we use information

  • Provide and secure the service.
  • Authenticate requests and enforce wallet policies.
  • Generate wallet blueprints and infrastructure outputs.
  • Detect misuse, fraud, abuse and operational failures.
  • Improve reliability, documentation and product behavior.
  • Provide support and communicate about service changes.
  • Meet legal, tax, compliance or security obligations where applicable.

6. Blockchain data

Transactions submitted to public blockchains may become permanently public. Build-a-Wallet cannot delete or alter information already written to a public blockchain.

7. Service providers

We may rely on infrastructure, hosting, RPC, pricing, monitoring, analytics, payment, communication or security providers. Those providers may process information on our behalf under their own agreements and policies.

8. Cookies and local storage

The site may use browser storage or similar technologies to preserve drafts, settings, session state, security controls and product functionality. Where analytics or optional tracking technologies are added, applicable consent requirements should be implemented before use.

9. Retention

We retain information only as long as reasonably necessary for service operation, security, dispute resolution, legal obligations and legitimate business needs. Public blockchain records are outside our control and may persist indefinitely.

10. Security

We use technical and organizational safeguards appropriate to the service, including scoped credentials, credential hashing, encrypted local key custody, policy controls, operator separation, revocation, wallet freeze controls and execution safeguards. No system can guarantee absolute security.

11. Your choices

You may choose not to submit optional information, may revoke or rotate supported credentials, and may stop using the service. Requests concerning personal information may be sent through the contact method published on buildawallet.xyz.

12. Children

The service is not directed to children under 13, and users must satisfy applicable age and legal-capacity requirements in their jurisdiction.

13. International use

If you access the service from outside the United States, information may be processed in countries with different data-protection laws.

14. Changes

We may update this policy as the product, law or infrastructure changes. The effective date above indicates the current version.

15. Contact

For privacy questions, use the contact information published on buildawallet.xyz.
